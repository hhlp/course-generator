ENGLISH GRAMMAR — A2

Primary grammar reference: Basic Grammar in Use — Raymond Murphy

Scope:
A2 grammar only. This path focuses on grammar, grammatical form, meaning, contrasts,
examples, common errors and controlled practice. It does not define separate reading,
listening, speaking or writing skills.

Progression rule:
A2 = introduce and establish.
Topics introduced here may return at B1–C2 with greater depth, complexity, exceptions
and grammatical analysis.
