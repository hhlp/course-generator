# Bibliography — English Grammar A2

## Principal

### Raymond Murphy — *Basic Grammar in Use*

Primary reference for the A1–A2 grammar stage.

Use it as the main pedagogical reference for:
- present and past forms;
- Present Perfect;
- passive structures appropriate to the level;
- verb forms and future forms;
- modal and auxiliary verbs;
- questions;
- `there` and `it`;
- `-ing` and `to`;
- pronouns and possessives;
- countable/uncountable nouns;
- articles, determiners and quantifiers;
- adjectives and adverbs;
- word order;
- conjunctions/clauses;
- prepositions;
- common structures with go, get, do, make and have.

The learning path is thematic and progressive; it does not need to reproduce the book's
unit order.

## Progression references for later levels

### Raymond Murphy — *English Grammar in Use*

Primary reference planned for B1–B2. Use later to consolidate and extend structures
introduced at A2, rather than duplicating A2 lessons.

### Martin Hewings — *Advanced Grammar in Use*

Primary reference planned for C1–C2. Use later for advanced structures, alternatives,
nuance, exceptions and grammatical analysis.

## Reference policy

- Do not invent page or unit numbers.
- Give exact unit/page references only when verified against the specific edition being used.
- Do not copy exercises or substantial copyrighted passages.
- Explain grammar independently and create original examples and exercises.
- Bibliographic references guide coverage; they do not limit the path when a prerequisite
  or grammatical distinction is needed for coherent learning.


## Additional A2 mapping

The A2 path also explicitly covers areas visible in the principal reference such as:
- indirect questions and their word order;
- basic `would rather`;
- two-object patterns such as `Give me the book / Give it to me`;
- phrasal verbs, including particle position.

These are integrated into the thematic path rather than reproducing the book's unit order.
