# Bibliography — English Grammar A2 — Deep Path

## Principal

### Raymond Murphy — *Basic Grammar in Use*, Fourth Edition

Primary pedagogical reference and backbone for the selected A2 grammar curriculum. The publisher describes the book as
American English, A1–B1; select content rather than treating its full contents as A2.

Use it to support:
- present and past forms;
- Present Perfect;
- passive structures appropriate to A2;
- verb forms and future forms;
- modal and auxiliary verbs;
- questions;
- `there` and `it`;
- `-ing` and `to`;
- pronouns and possessives;
- countable/uncountable nouns;
- articles, determiners and quantifiers;
- adjectives and adverbs: types, formation, position, comparison and adjective order;
- adverb categories and placement: frequency, manner, place, time, degree, probability and focus;
- word order;
- conjunctions and basic clauses;
- prepositions;
- high-frequency grammatical patterns.

The learning path is thematic and progressive. It does not reproduce the book's unit order.

## Supporting grammatical reference

The course may use reputable descriptive/reference grammar to clarify terminology, sentence
anatomy, form/meaning distinctions, constraints and error analysis, but all explanations must
remain pedagogically appropriate to CEFR A2.

Supporting references must not be used to import advanced structures into A2.

## Progression references for later levels

### Raymond Murphy — *English Grammar in Use*, Fifth Edition

Primary reference planned for B1–B2. Use later to consolidate and extend structures already
introduced at A2 rather than duplicating the A2 lessons.

### Martin Hewings — *Advanced Grammar in Use*, Fourth Edition

Primary reference planned for C1–C2. Use later for advanced structures, alternatives,
constraints, exceptions, nuance and advanced grammatical analysis.

## Deep-path reference policy

Bibliography supports both coverage and depth. For an A2 grammar point, references may guide:
- grammatical terminology;
- sentence anatomy;
- form and pattern;
- grammatical meaning and use;
- restrictions;
- contrasts;
- transformations;
- common errors;
- controlled original practice.

Depth must remain inside the A2 level boundary.

## Adjectives and adverbs — expanded A2 mapping

The principal reference supports the basic adjective/adverb distinction, adjective position,
common adjective sequences, -ly adverbs, frequency, comparisons and elementary sentence order.
The expanded path adds original, controlled lessons on:

- adjective categories and the typical opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material → purpose sequence;
- attributive/predicative adjectives and introductory coordination;
- adverb categories, formation, exceptions and comparison;
- initial, mid and final adverb placement, including frequency, auxiliaries and be;
- the common manner → place → time adverbial order, with appropriate flexibility;
- identification, reordering and error diagnosis.

Advanced classification labels (e.g. coordinate/cumulative, gradable/non-gradable, scope)
are introductory only, using simple A2 examples. They are not a licence to introduce
advanced syntax or discourse analysis. These categories and sequences are tendencies,
not inviolable rules; do not teach unnatural adjective strings as normal speech.

## Additional A2 mapping

The A2 path explicitly covers:
- grammar foundations and sentence anatomy;
- main verb vs auxiliary;
- introductory tense/aspect/voice/modality terminology;
- frequent indirect-question patterns and declarative word order;
- basic `would rather`;
- two-object patterns such as `Give me the book / Give it to me`;
- phrasal verbs and particle position;
- structural analysis;
- grammatical troubleshooting;
- cumulative checkpoints;
- final A2 integration and A2 → B1 boundary.

## Core vs optional expansion

Optional recognition notes retain existing coverage without claiming productive A2
mastery of future/modal/continuous passive, reporting backshift and reported-question
conversions, whom, or advanced modification terminology. Systematic development is
deferred. Follow domain.md and profile.yaml when a general coverage statement above
could be read more broadly. No optional topic is a compulsory checkpoint target.

American English is the default; valid labelled British alternatives are accepted.
Exact edition/unit/page references for later-level books require verification before
use. Those books are progression references, not required reading for this A2 course.
